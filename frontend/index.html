About DiaTrust-NX Architecture & Research

Technical specifications, duplicate-profile grouping formulation, dataset provenance, and clinical regulatory notices.

1. The Duplicate Profile Leakage Problem

Survey and questionnaire medical datasets naturally have patients with identical feature combinations. In UCI 529, 72.3% of rows (376/520) are duplicate predictor profiles. Standard randomized splitting puts identical profiles into both train and test splits, leading to artificial score inflation (>92.8%).

DiaTrust-NX uses deterministic profile group hashing: $$\forall x_i \in \text{Test}, \quad \nexists x_j \in \text{Train} \cup \text{Val} \quad \text{such that } x_i = x_j$$ This strictly prevents memorization and tests true generalization to unseen phenotypes.

2. The CDC Class Imbalance Breakthrough

In the CDC BRFSS population (253,680 records), positive diabetes incidence is only 13.9%. The legacy baseline suffered an unacceptably low recall of 58.18%, missing over 41% of diabetic individuals.

By integrating balanced loss weighting with validation-optimized thresholding ($\tau = 0.5136$), DiaTrust-NX achieves 78.70% recall (+20.52% gain), capturing nearly 8 out of 10 diabetic cases on unseen test data.

Mandatory Medical & Regulatory Disclaimer

DiaTrust-NX is a machine learning research prototype engineered strictly for academic, scientific, and experimental evaluation purposes. It has not been cleared or approved by the US FDA, EMA, or any regulatory medical authority. Predictions reflect statistical risk estimations and must not replace professional clinical evaluation, medical consultation, or certified laboratory testing (such as Fasting Plasma Glucose, Oral Glucose Tolerance Test, or Glycated Hemoglobin HbA1c assays).
